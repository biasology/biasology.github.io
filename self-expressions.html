Self-Expressions of the Misnamed

A living collection of creative works from people whose experiences have been misconstrued, pathologized, documented over, or institutionally flattened. Biasology names the wound; these works preserve the voice that survived it.

Plain Sight

by Ashfires

A soulful ballad about the frustration and pain of feeling unseen and misunderstood by those closest to you during mental health struggles. Written from the perspective of a witness who sees the quiet battles, silent screams, and heartbreak of emotional abandonment, Plain Sight becomes a call to listen deeper and care harder.
